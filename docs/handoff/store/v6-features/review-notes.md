1.2.0 resubmission. All changes below are server-side and already live in the current build; no new binary is needed.
1) 3.1.1 pricing link on the login screen: the iPhone/iPad app no longer offers any third-party login (those buttons opened a browser sheet in which our public website, including its pricing section, could be reached). The app has email + password login only, and no screen in the app leads to pricing, free-trial or purchase pages. Any page of ours opened from the app is rendered without pricing or sign-up content.
2) 3.1.1 business sign-up: the app has no sign-up or company-registration flow. Accounts are created on the web by a company administrator's invitation. In the app, sign-up and marketing URLs redirect to the login screen.
3) 2.3.6 age rating: the questionnaire was updated (Messaging and Chat: Yes).
4) 5.1.1(i) / 5.1.2(i) third-party AI: after login a consent screen "AI 기능 사용 안내" (About using AI features) is shown. Its first lines state the purpose and the recipient (the AI company Anthropic). "자세히 보기" (See details) expands what is sent (text/voice the user gives to the AI, attached files/photos, the user's own tasks/schedule/documents needed for the answer, library files when they are automatically sorted), who receives it (Anthropic, USA), why, and what happens if the user declines (only AI features are unavailable). Buttons: "AI 기능 쓰기" (Use AI features), "쓰지 않기" (Do not use), "나중에 정하기" (Decide later). Until the user agrees, our server rejects every AI request. The choice can be changed in 설정 (Settings) > 개인정보·AI. Privacy policy: https://www.work-in.dev/privacy (section 5).
To verify: log in with the demo account, the consent screen appears. Tap "나중에 정하기", open the AI assistant and send a message: the prompt appears again and no answer is produced. Tap "AI 기능 쓰기" and send again: the assistant answers.
5) 2.1(a) Sign in with Apple on iPad: Sign in with Apple was removed from the iPhone/iPad app together with all other third-party login options, so that path no longer exists.
Account deletion (5.1.1(v)): open Settings (profile picture at the top right > 설정·알림) and tap the red "계정 삭제" (Delete account) link at the top, or scroll to 계정 (Account) > 계정 삭제. The demo account can delete itself there after entering its password and the confirmation text. Accounts created by a company administrator send a deletion request from the same place.
Demo account: the user name and password are in the Sign-In Information fields (review organization with sample company data, administrator role).
